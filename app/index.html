OutboundRun help

What each button does

  • ↻ Refresh — full pass on an account: team, contacts, research, GTM hypothesis, and per-persona emails. 30-90 seconds.
  • ↻ Refresh Research — re-fetches the research summary only (SEC filings, earnings, news). Cheaper and faster.
  • ↻ Refresh GTM — regenerates the value hypothesis from existing research (does not re-fetch web).
  • + Find Contacts — asks Claude to identify additional targets at the account, deduped against existing contacts.
  • Send Email — copies the drafted email to your clipboard. OutboundRun never sends on your behalf.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • / — focus the search bar
  • ? — open this help
  • Esc — close any open modal

Account pipeline

Each account has a status dropdown: New → In progress → Opportunity → Won (or Lost / Cold / Disqualified). Filter by status from the toolbar. Star accounts to keep them at the top.

CSV upload format

Required column: account_name. Optional: account_number, industry, website, crm_id, notes. Sample file is one click away in the upload dialog.

Export

⬇ Export CSV in the top nav downloads every account and every contact (with all flags, actions, drafts) as one CSV, ready to paste into your CRM.
