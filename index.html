Common questions

How is this different from Apollo, Outreach, or Clay?

Apollo and Outreach are contact-database and sequencing tools built for SDR volume. Clay is data enrichment. None of them research a single enterprise account at the depth OutboundRun does: SEC filings, earnings calls, tech-stack signals, org context, all cited. We're the research + drafting layer that sits between your CRM and your inbox, purpose-built for enterprise AEs who need every touch to count.

Does OutboundRun replace my SDR or AE team?

No. OutboundRun replaces the 4-6 hours of research your reps skip because they don't have time. Your reps still send, still take the calls, still close. We give them the ammo to walk in warm.

What CRM integrations do you support?

Salesforce (Org62 SOQL) today. HubSpot and Pipedrive on the roadmap. You can also run OutboundRun in web-research-only mode with no CRM connection. Upload a CSV and go.

What happens to our data?

OutboundRun is local-first. Your account list, research output, and drafted emails live on the machine that runs it. Nothing hits our servers unless you explicitly send. Enterprise-friendly by default. No data-residency conversations to have with your security team.

How does the 10-15% reply rate compare to what we see today?

Industry cold-outreach reply rates run 1-3% (per Backlinko and QuickMail 2024 industry reports). OutboundRun is designed to hit 10-15% by grounding every email in specific, verifiable, per-persona detail that generic outreach can't touch. Design-partner data will validate the range as we roll out. We're honest about the "designed for" framing until we have the sample size.

Can I white-label OutboundRun for our team?

Design partners can. Every element of the identity (company name, product catalog, ICP, target roles, email signature) is set once and inherited by every prompt. There's no OutboundRun branding in what your reps send.
