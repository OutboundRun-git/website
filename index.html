How it works

01

Upload your account list

Drop a CSV or connect your CRM. Any industry, any product line, any target roles — configurable to your business.

02

OutboundRun researches every account

SEC 10-K filings, business news, tech stack signals, org context. Every fact cited. Nothing invented.

03

Per-persona outreach, drafted for your review

4-5 contacts per account. Each email grounded in a specific hook from the research. Green/yellow/red flagged for deliverability + tone.

04

You review. You send.

Human-in-the-loop. Nothing auto-fires. Your reputation stays yours.

What’s in the box

Research grounded in real sources

Every claim in every email traces back to a specific 10-K passage, news story, or company-website page. Inline citations in the research view. No hallucinated facts.

Configurable to your business

Your identity, your product catalog, your ICP, your target roles. No hardcoded industry. Set it once in the setup wizard and every prompt inherits it.

Per-persona outreach, not spray-and-pray

The message a CDO reads is different from the one the CFO reads. OutboundRun writes both, with pain-specific hooks per role.

CRM integration (Salesforce today, more coming)

Salesforce Org62 SOQL supported today. HubSpot and Pipedrive on the roadmap. Or run web-research-only with zero CRM setup.

Send from your own inbox

Copy-to-clipboard by default so your emails come from your domain, not a shared IP pool. Gmail integration optional.

Runs on your machine

Local-first architecture. Your account list, your research, your outreach — never leaves your laptop unless you send it.
