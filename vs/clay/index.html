OutboundRun vs Clay.

Clay is an enrichment and agent-workflow platform built for RevOps teams building data pipelines. OutboundRun is a per-account research and drafting tool built for the individual enterprise AE who has to work the resulting list. Different buyers, different jobs. Here is the honest breakdown.

Honest strengths of each.

Where OutboundRun wins
  • Depth per account, not breadth per row. Every account gets a full research summary and value hypothesis. Clay is optimized for enriching thousands of rows quickly.
  • AE-first workflow. A rep working their patch does not want to build waterfall workflows. OutboundRun assumes the user is the seller, not the ops person.
  • Cited every time. Facts trace to a specific 10-K passage or earnings-call quote. Clay does research but does not enforce citations.
  • Local-first, security-friendly. Your account list and drafts never leave the machine. Clay is cloud SaaS.
Where Clay wins
  • Enrichment at scale. Clay's waterfall (try Apollo, fall back to ZoomInfo, then Clearbit) is best-in-class for hitting a coverage target across a large list.
  • Team workflows. The spreadsheet UI is designed for RevOps to build a pipeline that runs on a schedule. OutboundRun is per-seat, per-session.
  • Agent orchestration. Clay lets you chain AI agents together with conditional logic. OutboundRun is one focused pipeline, not a workflow builder.
  • Integrations breadth. Native connectors to every enrichment provider on the market.
When to pick which

The short answer.

Pick Clay if you have a RevOps function building enrichment and lead-scoring workflows for the sales team. Clay is the category leader for that job.

Pick OutboundRun if you are the AE (or you run a team of AEs) working named accounts, and the problem is not enrichment but knowing what to say per account. OutboundRun is built for the seller who reads the CDO's earnings-call comments before the first touch.

They can coexist. RevOps uses Clay to build the enriched account list, then hands it to the AE who uses OutboundRun to do the per-account research + drafting before reaching out.
